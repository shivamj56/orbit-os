Debloat Google TV and Android TV without a computer

By Shivam Anil Jha, who makes Orbit OS · 16 November 2026 · 7 minute read

Short answer: debloating a TV means switching off the preinstalled apps you never use, so they stop taking memory, waking up at every start and showing promotions. Switch them off; don't delete them. A switched-off app comes back with one command, and a factory reset brings everything back. Below is the list Orbit OS uses on a tested TV, what never to touch, the commands for doing it by hand, and the no-computer way.

Switch off, don't uninstall

Android has two ways to get rid of a preinstalled app for your user:

  • pm disable-user --user 0 <package> switches the app off. It stays on the TV but never runs. pm enable <package> turns it back on.
  • pm uninstall --user 0 <package> removes it for your user. It is harder to get back, and nothing about it is faster than switching off.

Use the first one. Orbit OS only ever switches apps off.

Only if you say so

Orbit asks before touching these, because some homes need them:

  • Casting: com.google.android.apps.mediashell (Chromecast built-in). Keep it if you cast from your phone.
  • Voice search: com.google.android.katniss (Google Assistant). Without it, the remote's microphone button stops answering.
  • USB playback: the TV's own video player and gallery, for films and photos on a USB drive.
  • The maker's free channels: on Xiaomi, Xiaomi TV+.
  • The stock home screen: only together with another home screen. See removing ads from the Google TV home screen.

Doing it by hand with a computer

  1. Turn on Developer options and USB debugging on the TV.
  2. Install Android's platform tools on the computer, then connect to the TV over the Wi-Fi and press Allow on the TV: 
    adb connect 192.168.1.20:5555        # your TV's address
adb shell pm list packages -s        # the TV's system apps
  3. Switch apps off one at a time, and write each one down: 
    adb shell pm disable-user --user 0 com.google.android.feedback
  4. To see what is switched off, or to turn one back on: 
    adb shell pm list packages -d
adb shell pm enable com.google.android.feedback

Doing it from your phone with Orbit OS

Orbit OS does the same thing over your home Wi-Fi with no computer and no commands. It reads the TV's app list, asks five plain questions (do you cast, use voice search, play from USB…), shows the whole plan, writes down every change first and can back up the TV's original setup to your own Google Drive. It also puts a calm home screen and about 30 free Hindi Live TV channels on the TV. Undo turns every app back on with one tap.

It is tested and in daily use on a Xiaomi TV A Pro with Google TV 14. On other Google TV and Android TV sets it offers only the changes it knows are safe on any TV. It is not for Samsung, LG, Roku or Fire TV.

Questions

Will debloating make my TV faster?

It frees memory and stops apps from waking at every start, which most people notice on cheaper TVs. It can't make a slow chip fast, and the gain differs from TV to TV.

Is it the same as rooting?

No. Switching apps off uses Android's own developer settings. Nothing is unlocked, flashed or deleted.

What if something stops working?

Turn that app back on (pm enable, or Undo in Orbit). If you lost track, a factory reset brings every preinstalled app back.
