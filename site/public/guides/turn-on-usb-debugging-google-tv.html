Turn on Developer options and USB debugging on Google TV and Android TV

By Shivam Anil Jha, who makes Orbit OS · 16 November 2026 · 3 minute read

Short answer: open Settings › System › About, select Android TV OS build and press OK seven times. Then open System › Developer options and turn on USB debugging. When a phone app or a computer connects, the TV asks "Allow USB debugging?". Tick Always allow and press OK.

On Google TV

  1. Open Settings (top right of the home screen), then System › About.
  2. Scroll to Android TV OS build and press OK on it seven times. The TV counts down and then says you are a developer.
  3. Go back to System. Developer options is now there.
  4. Open it and turn on USB debugging.

On older Android TV menus

Many Android TV sets from before Google TV put the same things under Settings › Device Preferences: press OK seven times on About › Build, then find Developer options in Device Preferences. Menu names differ a little between makers; the seven presses always go on the build number.

Allowing a phone or computer

Despite its name, USB debugging also lets a phone app or a computer on the same Wi-Fi connect to the TV. The first time one does, the TV shows "Allow USB debugging?" with a key fingerprint. Tick Always allow from this computer and press OK. Nothing can connect until you press Allow on the TV itself.

When it doesn't work

No Developer options after pressing seven times

Make sure you pressed on the build number (Android TV OS build on Google TV, Build on older menus), and press until the TV says you are a developer.

My TV only has "Wireless debugging"

Some newer devices offer only Wireless debugging, which uses a pairing code. Orbit OS doesn't support those yet. If your TV is one of them, please tell us the model.

The phone can't find the TV

The phone and the TV must be on the same Wi-Fi, and the TV must be on, not in standby. Some routers keep devices apart ("guest" or "AP isolation" networks); use the main network.

Switch it off when you are done

Leaving USB debugging on is not dangerous in itself, because only devices you allowed can connect. Still, switch it off once you have finished: open System › Developer options and turn off USB debugging. Orbit's phone remote keeps working without it. Orbit asks you to turn it on again only when you change the setup or use Undo.

Set up your TV from your phone

With USB debugging on, Orbit OS can give your TV a calm home screen, free Hindi Live TV and a phone remote, and undo it all with one tap. It is free, open source (GPL-3.0) and has no ads or trackers.
