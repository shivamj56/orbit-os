Xiaomi TV: remove ads, PatchWall promotions and clutter

By Shivam Anil Jha, who makes Orbit OS · 16 November 2026 · 5 minute read

Short answer: on Xiaomi and Redmi TVs with Google TV or Android TV, the ads come from two places: Google TV's own sponsored banner, and Xiaomi's apps (PatchWall's promoted rows, Mi Channel promotions and a promoted tab). Settings can't switch either off. Switching off the Xiaomi promotion apps and using a different home screen removes them. Orbit OS does that from your phone. It is tested every day on a Xiaomi TV A Pro, and one tap undoes it.

The Xiaomi apps behind the promotions

On a Xiaomi TV A Pro with Google TV 14, these are the Xiaomi packages that show promotions, send reports or pop up on their own. Your model may have some of them, or similar ones:

The banner at the very top belongs to Google TV's own home screen, not to Xiaomi. Only a different home screen removes it. Here's how that works.

Keep these Xiaomi apps

  • com.mitv.livetv: inputs and HDMI. Your set-top box goes through it.
  • com.mitv.overseaservice: without it, the remote loses its pairing after standby.
  • Xiaomi TV+ (com.mitv.tvhome.mitvplus), if you watch its free channels. Orbit asks you first.
  • The TV's video player and gallery, if you play films or photos from a USB drive. Orbit asks about these too.

From your phone with Orbit OS

  1. Install Orbit OS from Google Play on your Android phone, on the same Wi-Fi as the TV.
  2. On the TV, open Settings › System › About, select Android TV OS build and press OK seven times. Then, in System › Developer options, turn on USB debugging.
  3. In Orbit, pick the TV and press Allow on the TV once.
  4. Answer five plain questions: casting, USB playback, voice search, Xiaomi's free channels, and ads and tracking. Orbit shows the whole plan before it changes anything.

On the tested TV it switches off 32 apps (never deletes them), puts Orbit Home there as a calm home screen of only your apps, and adds about 30 free Hindi Live TV channels from the broadcasters' own streams. The stock home screen is switched off only after Orbit Home has started, and every change is written down first. Settings › This TV › Undo brings PatchWall and every app back.

By hand with a computer

With USB debugging on and Android's platform tools on a computer:

adb connect 192.168.1.20:5555        # your TV's address
adb shell pm disable-user --user 0 com.mitv.tvhome.atv
adb shell pm disable-user --user 0 com.mitv.tvhome.michannel
adb shell pm disable-user --user 0 com.mitv.tvhome.oemtab

Write down every package you switch off. adb shell pm enable <package> turns one back on. The full list, with what never to touch, is in the debloat guide.

Questions

Does this remove YouTube ads?

No. Ads inside YouTube come from YouTube itself.

Will a Xiaomi update bring the ads back?

An update can add new preinstalled apps. If new promotions appear, run Orbit's setup again from Settings › This TV and review the new plan.

Is my warranty safe?

Orbit doesn't root the TV, unlock it or change its firmware; it uses Android's own developer settings and switches apps off, never deletes them. The warranty terms are Xiaomi's, so before a repair, use Undo first and the TV is just as it came.
